Manali


Manali is one of my favorite holiday destinations in India. It is known for its beautiful mountains, pleasant weather, green valleys, and exciting adventure activities. It is a perfect place to relax, enjoy nature, and spend quality time with family and friends.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley — famous for paragliding, zorbing, and snow activities.
  2. Rohtang Pass — a high mountain pass with breathtaking snow-covered views.
  3. Hadimba Temple — an ancient wooden temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali — known for its quaint cafes and relaxed vibe.
  5. Jogini Waterfall — a scenic trek leading to a refreshing waterfall.

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu — a steamed bread stuffed with local fillings.
  • Trout Fish — freshly caught and cooked in local spices.
  • Babru — a local version of kachori, popular street food.
  • Dham — a traditional multi-course Himachali feast.
  • Momos — steamed dumplings served with spicy chutney.
