holiday destination


Manali is one of my favorite holiday destinations in India. It is known for its beautiful mountains, pleasant weather, green valleys, and exciting adventure activities. It is a perfect place to relax, enjoy nature, and spend quality time with family and friends.

  1. Solang Valley - Famous for adventure sports.
  2. Rohtang Pass - Known for its snow-covered mountains.
  3. Hadimba Temple - A beautiful ancient temple.
  4. Old Manali - Popular for cafes and scenic views.
  5. Jogini Waterfall - A peaceful natural waterfall.
  • Siddu - A traditional Himachali dish.
  • Trout Fish - A popular local delicacy.
  • Babru - A tasty Himachali snack.
  • Dham - A traditional festive meal.
  • Momos - A favorite street food in Manali.
